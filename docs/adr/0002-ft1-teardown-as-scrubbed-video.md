# The FT-1 teardown is a scroll-scrubbed generated video, not a separable 3D model

The teardown that plays as you scroll is a Higgsfield video made between two frames, "assembled" and "exploded", from the same angle. Scrolling scrubs through it. A light procedural three.js FT-1 is used only for the drag-to-rotate moment and the small transmitter in the Channel UI. We chose this because a photoreal exploded view is worth more on stage than true part-by-part geometry. AI 3D generators output one fused mesh that can't be taken apart, and hand-modelling seven separable parts doesn't fit the timeline.

## Consequences

The scrubbed teardown and the rotatable 3D FT-1 are two different renderings of the same instrument. They must share proportions, colours and the lime accent so they read as one object. The teardown's camera angle is fixed, so labels for the seven layers are placed over the video at fixed scroll progress points, not attached to geometry.
