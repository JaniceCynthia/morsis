# Morsis

Morsis is a fictional interspecies signal research lab. This project is its public-facing identity and single landing page: credible field science, delivered with wonder. Tone is precise and warm; the first audience is curious dreamers, with rigour a scientist would respect.

## The lab and its instruments

**Morsis**:
The research lab itself — records, decodes and (under protocol) answers animal signals. Never a product company.
_Avoid_: brand, app, startup, pet company

**Decode-3**:
Morsis's translation model; it turns animal signals into English and composes replies from a species' own call units. Its output is always a hypothesis with a confidence, never a quotation.
_Avoid_: the AI, chatbot, translator

**FT-1**:
The Morsis field transmitter: a rugged, worn field radio a researcher uses to listen, segment, map meaning and emit. Its parts are grouped into the four Layers.
_Avoid_: device, gadget, collar, radio (in copy)

**Layer**:
One of the FT-1's four working stages, in order: Listen, Segment, Map meaning, Emit. Each Layer is carried by one or two physical parts inside the FT-1.
_Avoid_: step, module, stage

**Field station**:
One of Morsis's recording sites, numbered (e.g. Field station 07), spanning sky to seabed.

**Field kit**:
The set of identity touchpoints shown as real objects in use in the field (FT-1 case, field jacket, buoy livery, ID badge, Field Console icon, social post).
_Avoid_: merch, swag

**Field Console**:
The researchers' software tool whose app icon is a touchpoint. A research instrument, not a consumer app.
_Avoid_: pet app, Morsis app

## Animals and habitat

**Zone**:
One of three habitats on the page — Sky, Land, Sea — each with its own sound medium and band.

**Species**:
One of the six studied animals: raven and starling (Sky), elephant and prairie dog (Land), humpback whale and bottlenose dolphin (Sea).
_Avoid_: pet, creature

**Hero species**:
The three species (raven, elephant, humpback) that get cinematic video moments; the other three get stills and 3D only.

**Individual**:
A specific animal within a species, distinguished by its Voiceprint (e.g. two different humpbacks in one Eavesdrop).

**Specimen**:
A species as presented for inspection in the Specimen atlas — 3D model, key traits, habitat, signal sample.

## Signals and sound

**Signal**:
A single emitted call or song, made of one or more Units.
_Avoid_: bark, noise, message (for the animal side)

**Unit**:
The smallest segmentable piece of a Signal — a click, whistle, moan, chirp or rumble.
_Avoid_: syllable, word

**Contour**:
The pitch shape of a Signal over time, shown on the spectrogram and used to shape what you hear.

**Voiceprint**:
The fixed acoustic identity of an Individual — its pitch offset and timbre — so every reply sounds like that animal and no other.

**Hearing shift**:
The listener's octave dial that moves a Signal up or down so out-of-range calls (elephant infrasound, dolphin ultrasound) become audible.

## Conversations

**Channel**:
A researcher-to-animal conversation: the visitor types, the FT-1 encodes it into the species' Units, and Decode-3 returns the animal's reply. "Open a channel" is the action.
_Avoid_: chat, DM

**Eavesdrop**:
An intercepted animal-to-animal exchange between Individuals who could plausibly hear each other; the visitor listens and may interject or steer the topic.
_Avoid_: animal chat, group chat

**Exchange**:
One complete back-and-forth within a Channel or an Eavesdrop.

**Decoded reply**:
Decode-3's English rendering of a Signal, always paired with a Confidence and a Field note.

**Confidence**:
Decode-3's stated certainty in a Decoded reply.

**Field note**:
A true, citable behavioural fact attached to a Decoded reply, grounding it in published research.

## Page narrative

**The descent**:
The page's spine — one continuous camera move from 1,200 m up to 40 m below the sea, passing through the three Zones.

**Specimen atlas**:
The 3D inspection view for each Species.

**Evidence**:
The real, peer-reviewed findings Morsis builds on, cited by author, journal and year.
