# Morsis — Wonderbuilds26' Track 1

## About the company

Morsis is a software research company working on **translating human speech into signals animals can understand**. Think of them as building the first real interspecies communication layer. They're **serious scientists**, but what they're making is inherently **magical**, and they know it.

## The problem

Nobody knows they exist. When people hear about them, they either don't believe it or think it's a gimmick. Morsis needs a public-facing identity that makes their work feel **credible, exciting, and a little bit wondrous**. They want to be a well-known research organization/lab.

## What they are NOT

- Not a pet gadget company
- Not a toy brand
- No collars, no apps that "bark back" as a joke
- They are a **research lab**, not a physical product manufacturer

### 2. Landing page

- [ ] A **single landing page** that explains what Morsis does in an entertaining way
- [ ] Just one page

### 3. Interactive element

- [ ] **At least one interactive element** embedded in the landing page
- [ ] Something the visitor *plays with*, not just watches
- [ ] Must work in the browser at demo time

#### Interaction ideas (from the brief)

- Type a sentence, hear or see it "translated" into dog, whale, or bird
- A live conversation visualizer between two species
- A frequency slider that morphs human voice into animal calls
- Hold a key to "speak" and watch an animal respond
