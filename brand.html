Morsis · Interspecies Signal Lab · Identity

Serious science. Inherently wondrous.

One mark, five colours and two typefaces. Precise where it carries facts, warm where it carries wonder, and built to survive the field: a jacket, a buoy, an instrument.

01 · Logo

A signal that resolves into a word.

The mark is one continuous line: a call rising, looping and settling, which is also an M. It always leads the wordmark and is never redrawn, outlined or stretched. On light grounds it is Field Ink; on dark it is Lab Paper, with Signal Lime saved for moments that should feel live.
